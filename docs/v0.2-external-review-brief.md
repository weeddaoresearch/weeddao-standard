# External review brief — WeedDAO Cannabis Data Record v0.2 Draft

**Audience:** growers, laboratories / LIMS practitioners, researchers, software developers, dispensary / product-data operators  
**Reading time:** about 2–3 minutes  
**Status:** EXTERNAL REVIEW CANDIDATE — experimental draft, **not** a release

## WHAT

WeedDAO Cannabis Data Record `v0.2-draft` is a **proposed JSON shape** for exchanging cannabis cultivation, harvest, product-batch, and lab-result records. It is a shared data language — not a product, not a certification program, not a regulation, and **not** a claim of industry adoption.

`v0.1-alpha` remains the **published frozen baseline**. `v0.2-draft` is the evidence-driven successor under independent review.

Branch: https://github.com/weeddaoresearch/weeddao-standard/tree/v0.2-draft

## WHY v0.2

A public COA corpus (50 cases / 8 jurisdictions / 7 labs / 40 primary / 7 secondary) showed that v0.1-alpha could not represent recurring real COA facts in **core** schema:

| Gap | Corpus count | GitHub issue |
|-----|-------------:|-------------:|
| ND / not detected | 48 | #3 |
| Concurrent multi-unit cannabinoids | 43 | #4 |
| Below-reporting-limit (`<LOQ` / `<LOD`) | 35 | #1 |
| Analyte-level NOT_PERFORMED / NOT_TESTED | 22 | #2 |

Additional recurring evidence (not Issues #1–#4, but relevant to draft scope):

| Topic | Corpus count |
|-------|-------------:|
| Sample lifecycle metadata | 27 |
| Regulatory tracking identifiers | 30 |

v0.2-draft also fixes subject semantics: finished products are not forced into `cultivation_batch_id`, and product trade names are not forced into `cultivar`.

## WHAT changed (high level)

- **Subject model** — required `subject` (`subject_type`, `subject_id`, optional product/matrix/external IDs)
- **AnalyteResult** — `result_state` separate from `assessment`; multi-unit `measurements[]`; explicit ND / below-limit / not-performed / not-reported
- **Sample lifecycle** — collected / received / tested / reported kept distinct when the source provides them
- **External identifiers** — open `scheme` + `value` (e.g. METRC, lab sample, producer lot)

## WHAT to evaluate (10 focused questions)

1. **Subject model** — Are `subject_type` + `subject_id` the right required identity for cultivation, harvest, product, and sample records?
2. **Cultivar vs product name** — Is “cultivar = genetics only; product trade names in `subject.name`” clear enough in practice?
3. **`result_state` vs `assessment`** — Does separating measurement state from pass/fail assessment match how labs and LIMS actually encode results?
4. **ND** — Is `result_state: "not_detected"` (without coercing to `0`) usable for your exports?
5. **Below-limit** — Does `below_reporting_limit` + `limits[]` / `reported_as` cover `<LOQ` / `<LOD` without sentinel zeros?
6. **Not performed / not tested / not reported** — Are these first-class states sufficient, or do you need finer distinctions?
7. **Multi-unit measurements** — Is `measurements[]` with concurrent units (e.g. `%` + `mg/g` + `mg/serving`) the right shape?
8. **Sample lifecycle** — Should `collected_at` / `received_at` / `tested_at` / `reported_at` stay strictly distinct (no silent substitution)?
9. **External identifiers** — Is an open `scheme`/`value` model workable for METRC and similar track-and-trace IDs?
10. **Visual / qualitative safety** — For “Passed Visual Inspection” with no numeric foreign-material value, is `result_state: "unknown"` + `assessment: "pass"` + `reported_as` acceptable (no numeric `0` sentinel)?
